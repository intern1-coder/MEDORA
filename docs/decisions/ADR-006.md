# ADR-006: Appointment-Aware Queue Scheduling and Clinical Urgency Priority

## Status
- **Appointment-Aware Queue Management**: **OWNER APPROVED**.
- **Walk-in Support**: **OWNER APPROVED**.
- **Authorized Clinical Urgency Priority**: **OWNER APPROVED**.
- **Hierarchical Queue Model**: **PROPOSED** (Engineering recommendation; numerical weight formula removed).
- **Runtime Implementation**: **NOT IMPLEMENTED** (No code written yet).
- **Future Triage Role (TRIAGE_NURSE)**: **PROPOSED / AWAITING DECISION** (Under current approved baseline, urgency assignment is restricted to `DOCTOR`).

## Context
Hospital outpatient departments face a dual-flow patient intake:
1. **Pre-booked Scheduled Appointments**: Patients who booked fixed time slots in advance and expect prompt service during their slot.
2. **Walk-in Patients**: Patients who arrive without prior booking and receive sequential daily queue tokens.

The project owner approved appointment-aware queue management, walk-in support, and authorized clinical urgency priority, while rejecting arbitrary numerical weighting formulas.

---

## 1. Confirmed Queue Principles [OWNER APPROVED]
- **Dual-Flow Balance**: Scheduled appointments and walk-in arrivals are managed in a unified, transparent queue.
- **Controlled Urgency Authority**: Under the approved 4-role baseline (`PATIENT`, `DOCTOR`, `RECEPTIONIST`, `HOSPITAL_ADMIN`), **only licensed DOCTOR** is authorized to assign or elevate clinical urgency (`URGENT` or `EMERGENCY`).
- **Receptionist Bar**: Receptionists are **strictly forbidden** from assigning clinical urgency. Receptionists issue standard walk-in tokens only.
- **Audit Mandate**: Any clinical urgency assignment requires a recorded medical justification and generates an immediate audit log entry.

---

## 2. Proposed Hierarchical Queue Model [PROPOSED / AWAITING OWNER REVIEW]
Rather than arbitrary point formulas, the queue engine prioritizes patient calls using a deterministic 5-tier hierarchical evaluation policy:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Tier 1: Clinical Urgency (Assigned by DOCTOR)                          │
│         - EMERGENCY / CRITICAL takes immediate head-of-line precedence │
│         - URGENT cases evaluated ahead of standard queue               │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 2: Appointment-Window Handling                                    │
│         - Scheduled patients who checked in within their 15-minute     │
│           slot window are called during their booked slot window       │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 3: Waiting-Time Fairness                                          │
│         - Within the same category, earlier check-in times take        │
│           precedence to prevent patient neglect                        │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 4: Walk-in Handling                                               │
│         - Standard walk-ins are interleaved into available time gaps   │
│           between appointment windows and during doctor schedule gaps  │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Tier 5: Missed-Call & Requeue Handling                                 │
│         - Called tokens not reporting within window (e.g. 3 calls /    │
│           10 mins) transition to MISSED                                │
│         - Re-queued patients are placed behind currently waiting       │
│           patients who reported on schedule                            │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## 3. Queue State Machine & Transitions

```
               ┌───────────────┐
               │    WAITING    │ ◄─── Walk-in issued OR Appointment checked-in
               └───────┬───────┘
                       │
             (Doctor calls next)
                       ▼
               ┌───────────────┐
               │    CALLED     │ ─── (3 calls / 10 min no-show) ──► [ MISSED ]
               └───────┬───────┘                                       │
                       │                                    (Re-queued by reception)
             (Patient enters room)                                     ▼
                       ▼                                         [ WAITING ]
               ┌───────────────┐                             (Placed behind waiting)
               │    ENGAGED    │ (Active consultation commenced)
               └───────┬───────┘
                       │
             (Consultation completed)
                       ▼
               ┌───────────────┐
               │    SERVED     │ (Final state)
               └───────────────┘
```

## 4. Triage Role Governance Recommendation [PROPOSED]
- **Current Approved Baseline**: The 4 approved roles remain `PATIENT`, `DOCTOR`, `RECEPTIONIST`, and `HOSPITAL_ADMIN`.
- **Engineering Recommendation**:
  - In a single-doctor or small clinic setting, restricting urgency assignment to `DOCTOR` is safe and manageable.
  - In a high-volume outpatient department, having doctors step out to triage walk-in patients creates bottlenecks.
  - We recommend evaluating an additional `TRIAGE_NURSE` role in a future phase (e.g., Phase 6 queue management), permitting certified clinical nursing staff to record pre-consultation vitals and elevate queue urgency at the intake desk with full audit logging.
  - Until approved by the owner, this role is **NOT** included in the active role matrix.

## Consequences & Trade-offs
- Provides a clean, transparent, and defensible ordering policy without black-box numerical weights.
- Strictly guards against non-clinical queue tampering while ensuring emergency patients receive immediate care.
