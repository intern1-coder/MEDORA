# ADR-005: Consultation Immutability and Clinical Addenda Pattern

## Status
- **Consultation Locking and Signed Addenda**: **OWNER APPROVED**.
- **Mandatory Lifecycle Order**: **OWNER APPROVED**.
- **Runtime Implementation**: **NOT IMPLEMENTED** (No code written yet).

## Context
Electronic Health Records (EHR) demand strict clinical and legal integrity. Once a consultation note, diagnosis, examination finding, or vital sign is finalized by a licensed consulting doctor, it becomes a legal clinical record. Silently modifying, editing, or deleting finalized clinical documentation introduces medical malpractice liabilities, compromises patient safety, and violates healthcare auditability standards.

---

## Technical Design & Mandatory Lifecycle Workflow

### 1. Mandatory Consultation Lifecycle Order [OWNER APPROVED]
A consultation record must **NEVER** be created merely because an appointment was booked, a patient checked in, a token was generated, or a patient entered the waiting area.

The strict chronological workflow is:
1. **Appointment is booked**: Status is `SCHEDULED`.
2. **Patient checks in**: Status is `CHECKED_IN`.
3. **Queue token is generated**: Queue token status is `WAITING`.
4. **Doctor calls the patient**: Token status transitions to `CALLED`.
5. **Doctor begins the actual consultation**: Patient enters the room; token transitions to `ENGAGED`, appointment transitions to `IN_CONSULTATION`.
6. **Consultation record is created**: The `Consultation` database row is instantiated **only at this exact moment**, linked to patient, doctor, appointment, and department. Status is `IN_PROGRESS`.
7. **Doctor records clinical details**: Vitals, chief complaints, physical examination, provisional/final diagnoses.
8. **Doctor finalises the consultation**: Status transitions to `FINALIZED`.
9. **Finalised consultation is locked**: The record becomes permanently immutable.
10. **Corrections are recorded through signed addenda**: Child records in `ConsultationAddendum`.

```
       [Doctor formally begins consultation]
                         │
                         ▼
       Status: IN_PROGRESS  ◄─── Doctor iteratively drafts notes, vitals, provisional diagnoses
                         │
                         │ (Doctor explicitly calls POST /api/v1/consultations/:id/finalize)
                         ▼
       Status: FINALIZED    ──── Locked! Original row becomes IMMUTABLE
                         │
                         ▼
     [Subsequent Correction Needed?]
                         │
                         ▼
     POST /api/v1/consultations/:id/addendum
     - Addendum text / clinical findings
     - Author Doctor ID + Timestamp
     - Mandatory justification reason
                         │
                         ▼
     Appended to [ConsultationAddendum] Table
     (Original Consultation record remains 100% untouched)
```

### 2. Immutability Enforcement [PROPOSED]
Immutability is enforced at both application and database layers:
1. **Application Service Guard**: Any `PUT` or `PATCH` targeting a consultation checks `if (consultation.status === 'FINALIZED') throw new ForbiddenError('Finalized clinical records cannot be modified. Use addenda.')`.
2. **Database Restrict Guard**: A PostgreSQL trigger prevents updates to clinical text columns when `status = 'FINALIZED'`.

### 3. Administrative Access Governance [OWNER APPROVED]
- Hospital Administrators have authorized read access across clinical consultations for operational governance and legal compliance.
- Every administrative read of confidential clinical data triggers an audit log (`CLINICAL_RECORD_ADMIN_OVERRIDE`).
- Hospital Administrators **cannot alter or delete** a doctor's finalized consultation notes, and cannot author clinical records. Any administrative note must follow the signed-addendum workflow.

## Consequences & Trade-offs
- Prevents premature consultation ghost records.
- Preserves complete chronological history of clinical observations without risk of silent tampering.
